## Zellij

### Prefix

 - Ctrl-a

### Navigation

 - Vim navigation style with Ctrl-h j k l

### Splits

 - Ctrl-a v opens a vertical split (right)
 - Ctrl-a s opens a horizontal split (down)
 - Ctrl-z toggles zoom out/in on the current pane

## Global

 - Ctrl-a d detaches current session
 - Ctrl-a e synchronizes all panes in tab
